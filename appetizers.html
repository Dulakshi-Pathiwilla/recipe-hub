Appetizer Recipes

1. Garlic Bread

Ingredients:

  • Baguette slices
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Parsley

Steps:

  1. Mix butter and minced garlic.
  2. Spread on bread slices.
  3. Bake for 10 mins until golden.

2. Mini Veggie Spring Rolls

Ingredients:

  • Spring roll wrappers
  • Mixed vegetables
  • Soy sauce
  • Oil for frying

Steps:

  1. Sauté vegetables with soy sauce.
  2. Wrap mixture in spring roll wrappers.
  3. Deep fry until golden brown.
