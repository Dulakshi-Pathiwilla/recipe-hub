Dessert Recipes

1. Chocolate Mousse

Ingredients:

  • Dark chocolate
  • Whipping cream
  • Eggs
  • Sugar

Steps:

  1. Melt the chocolate and let it cool.
  2. Whisk eggs and sugar until fluffy.
  3. Fold in whipped cream and chocolate.
  4. Chill for 2 hours before serving.

2. Fruit Trifle

Ingredients:

  • Sponge cake pieces
  • Custard
  • Mixed fruits
  • Whipped cream

Steps:

  1. Layer cake, fruits, and custard in a glass bowl.
  2. Top with whipped cream.
  3. Refrigerate for 1 hour and serve chilled.
